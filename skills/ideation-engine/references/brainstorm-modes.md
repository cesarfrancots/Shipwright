# Brainstorm Modes

## Divergent

Maximize variety.
Explore different user segments, delivery mechanisms, pricing models, and workflow changes.
Use when the problem space is broad or early.

## Convergent

Stay close to the stated problem and optimize around a narrow objective.
Use when direction is already chosen and the team needs focused options.

## Contrarian

Challenge the default assumptions.
Ask what happens if the usual constraints, pricing, audience, or channel choices are wrong.
Use when the current strategy feels crowded or stale.

## Adjacent

Borrow patterns from neighboring industries, products, or workflows.
Use when the team wants inspiration without pure blue-sky ideation.

# Audience Profiles

## C-Level

Focus on business outcome, timeline, revenue or customer impact, major risk, and the decision or support needed.

## Engineering

Focus on scope, dependencies, constraints, technical risks, rollout readiness, and unresolved questions.

## Design

Focus on user workflow, edge cases, experience tradeoffs, and handoff clarity.

## Sales

Focus on customer-facing value, objections, packaging, timing, and competitive framing.

## Support

Focus on what changed, who may ask about it, likely failure modes, and response guidance.

## Customers

Focus on value delivered, plain language, and any action required from them.
